The morning

how it goes.

you get there, warm up, and see who you're playing with.

i pair people by what they told me on the form. the person across the net might be building in the same space as you, or reading the same books, or trying to fix the same problem from the other side.

we play doubles and switch partners so you get time with everyone. after that, stick around and keep talking or head out. your call.

some weeks a member hosts at their own court, or runs a clinic, or leads a dinner on something they know a lot about. if you want to do one of those, tell me.

Good to know

before you come.

if it rains.

if the forecast says rain or the courts will be wet, that week is off. i'll email everyone by 9pm the night before, so check your inbox before you set an alarm.

what to bring.

your own racket and a can of balls, plus water and proper court shoes. bring a layer too, the park is often cold and foggy in the morning.

getting there.

goldman tennis center, 50 bowling green dr, at the east end of golden gate park. the 7 and 33 muni lines stop on stanyan nearby, and the n judah is a short walk. parking in the park fills up and may cost money, so leave a little early.

play at your own risk.

tennis is a sport, and people get hurt sometimes. look after yourself and play within your limits. by joining you agree to the terms & waiver and privacy policy.
